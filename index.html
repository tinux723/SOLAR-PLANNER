        Ask the installer before you pay

        1. Battery brand, model and cycle rating. Good LiFePO4 is rated 4,000+ cycles.
        2. Is the battery new? Ask for the serial number and the warranty card.
        3. Panel brand and wattage printed on the back label, not just the box.
        4. Warranty in writing: inverter, battery and workmanship, and who to call.
        5. An itemised quote: cables, breakers, surge protection, earthing, mounting and labour.
